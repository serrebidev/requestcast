Retries and pacing

Bulk downloads — a channel, a discography, or a playlist import — are what trip YouTube's rate limiting: a run of tracks fails with 403 or "video unavailable" even though the same tracks download fine later. These settings decide how hard and how patiently RequestCast tries again.

0 to 10. Each attempt asks YouTube as a different client, which is what usually clears a 403. Anything still failing gets one more pass at the end of the job.

0 to 600. The wait doubles with each further attempt.

0 to 120. A gap is the most effective way to stop a long queue being rate limited in the first place. Raise it if whole batches keep failing.

0 to 3600. Used when several downloads in a row are refused, and again before the final pass over the tracks that failed. 0 turns pausing off.

0 to 10. Applies when a download fails outright rather than track by track. Any finished download can also be queued again by hand from its status page.

Tool updates

yt-dlp, ffmpeg, and Deno are installed for you in the background when they are missing, whatever this setting says — nothing downloads without them.

Recommended. All three stop working as the sites they read change, and an out-of-date yt-dlp is the most common cause of 403 and "video unavailable" failures.
